A small mono status or tag label; one per story beside its number, one per prediction, one per quote tile.

```jsx
<Pill label="DO THIS" />  <Pill label="MISS" />  <Pill count={9} />
```

- IN PROGRESS and OPEN are mustard (--active), never red or accent. HIT/DONE green, MISS red.
- Colour is never the only cue: the label always shows.
