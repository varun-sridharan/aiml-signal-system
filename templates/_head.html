{# Real webfonts for every rendered page. WHY THIS EXISTS: the Claude Design bundles declare 34 @font-face rules whose `src` values are internal bundler UUIDs. Inside the bundle its runtime resolves them; served as a plain template they are 34 dead URLs, so /plan fell back to Georgia, the system sans and Menlo while /plan/Plan.html rendered in the real faces. The bundles already carry preconnect hints to fonts.googleapis.com with no stylesheet behind them — this is the stylesheet those hints were waiting for. The templates carry none of those dead rules: this link is the only font source on /plan and /roadmap. The weight list is not the full families. It mirrors exactly what the bundle declares, which is what Claude Design subset for these pages: Newsreader 400/500/600 plus 400 italic, Public Sans 400/500/600/700, IBM Plex Mono 400/500. Same 34 faces, same subsets. Mono at 600 (the status pills) is synthesised from 500 here exactly as it is in the bundle, so the pages render as the bundles did rather than looking heavier. #}
