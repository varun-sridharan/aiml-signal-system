Weekly Digest — slot list

The pipeline fills these slots and nothing else. Opt = optional. A section with nothing to show gets its one empty line, never a placeholder. Inline tokens inside prose: [[#s03|text]] links to a story tile, [[fn:key|Term]] places a footnote marker (Learning only). [[hl|phrase]] highlights one phrase of up to about five words in a section headline, in that section's colour: at most one per headline, and a headline without one renders entirely in ink.
