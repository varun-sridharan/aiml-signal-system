HTML comments in plan.md were dropped by the parser and never reach this template. ---- Status vocabulary (layout note, carried from Plan.json _statusVocabulary) ---- Four status values, and each should read as its own state rather than as a shade of alarm. DONE — Finished. Settled, not celebratory. Colour: --ok, outlined or lightly tinted. IN PROGRESS — Being worked on right now. Exactly one row carries this, and the milestone itself shows a percentage instead. Active is a neutral state, and the colour should say *here*, not *problem*. Colour: **Mustard, not red.** Fill `--active-soft` #F5EAD0 with `--active` #7A5F14 text and a 1px `--active` border — the same lightly-tinted treatment DONE gets in green, so the two read as members of one scale rather than as success and failure. **Do not use --accent or --warn here**; both are terracotta and both read as danger. TO DO — Planned, not started. Quiet. Colour: --faint, outlined. TO DO · blocks paid runs — Planned, not started, and it gates something. The only row with an ordering constraint. Colour: `--faint` outline for the TO DO half, and the blocker half in `--warn` terracotta with a warning mark. **This is the only place terracotta is correct on this page** — it is the one row where something really is at risk. ---- Palette addition (layout note, carried from Plan.json _paletteAddition) ---- Why: The palette had no colour for *active*. `--ok` is green for done, `--faint` grey for idle, and `--accent` and `--warn` are **the same terracotta** (#B5502A).
